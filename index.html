About the Candidate

I am a committed student leader of the College of Nursing Science, Madobi Campus, contesting for the office of Student Union Government President with a clear mission: to serve students with integrity, transparency, and responsibility.

Leadership to me is service — listening to students, representing their interests, and working tirelessly to improve campus life.

Academic Journey

Comr. Adam Ibrahim Alkali was born in 2003 in Tudun Wada Local Government Area of Kano State. His educational journey demonstrates resilience and commitment to learning through various institutions:

  • 2011-2017: Imam Malik Nursery and Primary School
  • 2017-2020: Imam Malik Science and Tahfiz College (Junior Secondary)
  • 2020-2023: Science College Dawakin Tofa (Senior Secondary)
  • 2023-2024: College of Advanced and Remedial Studies (CAS), Tudun Wada
  • 2024-Present: College of Nursing and Midwifery, Madobi Campus (Community Nursing)
