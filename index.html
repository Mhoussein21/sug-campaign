About the Candidate

I am a committed student leader of the College of Nursing Science, Madobi Campus, contesting for the office of Student Union Government President with a clear mission: to serve students with integrity, transparency, and responsibility.

Leadership to me is service — listening to students, representing their interests, and working tirelessly to improve campus life.

Academic Journey & Background

Comr. Adam Ibrahim Alkali was born in 2003 in Tudun Wada Local Government Area of Kano State. His educational path demonstrates resilience, adaptability, and a commitment to learning.

2009 - 2010 Early Education

Began nursery education at Model Primary School.

2011 - 2017 Primary Education

Transferred to Imam Malik Nursery and Primary School, where he completed his primary education.

2017 - 2020 Junior Secondary

Joined Junior Secondary School at Imam Malik Science and Tahfiz College.

2020 - 2023 Senior Secondary

After passing the Science and Technical Schools Board exam, he studied at Science College Dawakin Tofa (SS1A, Galadima House), completing WAEC and NECO examinations.

2023 - 2024 Remedial Studies

Studied at the College of Advanced and Remedial Studies (CAS), Tudun Wada.

2024 - Present Current Studies

Currently enrolled in a two-year Community Nursing program at the College of Nursing and Midwifery, Madobi Campus.
